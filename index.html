How we work

A Florida company, and not a substitute for professionals

Zebergs Consulting LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We are not accountants, attorneys, financial advisers or a recruitment agency. Tax filings, legal questions, employment law and investment decisions go to the appropriate licensed professional — we'll say when you've reached that point rather than offering an opinion we're not qualified to give.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do you do our accounts or taxes?

No. We're not a CPA firm. We work on pricing, costs and process, and we'll coordinate with your accountant where it helps.

Can you advise on firing someone?

No — employment law questions go to an employment attorney. We can help with role clarity and performance expectations, which often prevents reaching that point.

How long does it take to see a difference?

Pricing changes show up in the next invoices. Process and hiring work takes longer. We'll be honest about which is which rather than promising quick wins everywhere.

Will you just give us a report?

Only if that's the scope. Our preference is implementing one change and seeing it hold — reports alone rarely change anything.

Do you work with very small businesses?

Yes, most of our clients are owner-run with a handful of staff. Smaller businesses can change direction quickly, which makes the work more satisfying.

What does it cost?

Quoted in writing after a free first call, per project or at a day rate. Nothing is charged before you accept.
