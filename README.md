## Business description (Stripe, directories…)

```
Zebergs Consulting LLC, a Florida limited liability company, provides management consulting services to small business owners: reviewing pricing and margin, planning hiring and delegation as a business grows, putting systems and documented processes in place, and building practical plans with a small number of measures to track them. Work is scoped and quoted in writing after a free first call and billed per project or per day. The company is not a CPA firm, law firm, financial adviser or recruitment agency, and provides no tax, legal, investment or employment-law advice. Site: zebergsconsultingllc.shop
```
